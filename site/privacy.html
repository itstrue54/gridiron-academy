Gridiron Academy privacy policy

For the Gridiron Academy learner app on Android. Effective {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}.

The short version. Gridiron Academy collects no personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it can't reach the internet: the app doesn't ask Android for internet access. Everything it remembers stays on your device.

Who we are

Gridiron Academy is made by {{DEVELOPER}}. You can reach us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

What the app keeps on your device

To remember where a player is in the course, the app saves a file in its own private storage on the device:

  • a nickname the player or a grown-up picks (we ask for a nickname, never a real name);
  • an age range, such as 8-9, used to choose the kid or adult course;
  • progress: levels and drills passed, streaks, and a record of answers, used for review and practice;
  • random identifiers made on the device, which link that record together.

We never ask for a name, birthday, photo, email address, phone number or location. This file is not sent to us or to anyone else, and Android's cloud backup is turned off for the app, so it is not copied to a cloud account either.

Backup files

The grown-up area can save a backup of a player's progress as a file, and load one back. This only happens when a grown-up chooses it, and the file goes where they choose to save it. The app doesn't send it anywhere.

Children

Gridiron Academy is made for children and grown-ups alike. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. The parts of the app meant for grown-ups, including backups and the link to this policy, sit behind a grown-up check.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes everything it saved. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you put them until you delete them.

Other apps and services

The app shares nothing with other companies: no advertising networks, analytics services or data brokers. Google Play may collect information when you download the app, under Google's privacy policy. A separate Coach & Parent app is planned. It is not part of this app and will have its own privacy policy.

Changes

If the app ever starts to collect or share information, we will update this policy before it does, change the effective date above and, for anything about children, ask a parent first.
