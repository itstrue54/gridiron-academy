## Names (content rule 1)

System names that come from coaches or a team (Air Coryell, Erhardt-Perkins, "often
linked to Vic Fangio" in `ar8-defense-01`) are used only as the common label for a
system, with no biography or team branding. Confirm this sits within content rule 1
(no player names, logos or licensed material); if not, replace them with descriptive
names ("vertical numbered-tree offense", "two-high light-box defense").
