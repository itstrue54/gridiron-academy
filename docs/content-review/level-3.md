# Level 3: items for football review

Same process as Level 2 (`level-2.md`): validator-clean, but these need a coach's
sign-off before a public release.

## Conventions that vary by system

| Step | Claim | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `a3-tree-01`, `a3-tree-02`, `a3-tree-08`, glossary `route-tree` | One common tree: 1 flat, 2 slant, 3 comeback, 4 curl, 5 out, 6 dig, 7 corner, 8 post, 9 go; odd outside, even inside. | Numbering differs between systems (some use 0 for the hitch, some swap 1 and 3). The content says "one common tree" and "systems differ". |
| `a3-timing-02` | Quick game on three-step drops, curls/outs/digs on five-step, deep routes on longer drops or play-action. | A teaching generalization; shotgun offenses time it differently. |
| `a3-fronts-01`, `a3-fronts-02`, `k3-fronts-*` | 4-3 linemen attack single gaps; 3-4 linemen occupy blockers, outside linebackers rush. | True of the traditional versions; many modern fronts are hybrids. |
| `a3-shells-01`, `k3-safeties-02` | One-high usually means Cover 1 or 3; two-high usually means Cover 2 or 4. | Correct as families; disguise is taught at Level 7 and the content already says the shell is a clue. |

## Rules that change by season

| Step | Claim | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `a3-twominute-01`, `a3-twominute-03` | A first down doesn't stop the NFL clock; in college it stops briefly late in each half. | NCAA changed first-down clock rules in 2023 (now only in the last two minutes of each half). Re-check each offseason. |
| `a3-twominute-02` | Late in each half, the NFL clock stays stopped after a runner goes out of bounds until the snap. | The exact windows are the last two minutes of the first half and the last five minutes of the game. |

## Simplifications for kids

| Step | Simplification |
|---|---|
| `k3-man-01`, `k3-zone-01` | Man and zone presented as pure types; pattern-matching and combination coverages come at Level 6. |
| `k3-runs-03` | The counter is shown with one pulling lineman; many counters pull two. |
| `k3-smarts-04` | "Near the goal line, teams kick or go for it": no single right answer, so the item accepts both. |
