# Level 6: items for football review

Validator-clean and rendered; these need a coach's sign-off before release.

## Coverage variants (the read-the-defense items)

| Variant | Drawn as | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Cover 1 robber | Single-high, a safety sitting at about 10 in the middle | "Lurk" and "rat" are used for similar but not identical jobs; the content says so. |
| 2-man | Pressed man under two deep halves | |
| Tampa 2 | Cover 2 shell; the Mike runs the deep middle | |
| Cover 3 sky / buzz / cloud | Sky: SS to curl-flat. Buzz: SS to hook, Sam to flat. Cloud: corner to flat, safety to his deep third | Some staffs use "cloud" for any corner-flat call regardless of shell. |
| Quarters | Two safeties at 10, corners at 8, four deep quarters | Pattern-match rules simplified to "safeties read the slot". |
| Palms (`p6-quarters-02`) | Slot to the flat: corner jumps him, safety takes #1 deep | Also called 2-read or Cover 7 on some staffs; confirm the description. |
| Cover 6 | Halves on one side, quarters on the other | Which side gets which varies by formation and call. |

Left out on purpose: **MOD**, **Cover 9** and **Cover 7** as named coverages, because the
same names mean different coverages on different staffs. Decide whether to teach one
convention at Level 7 or 8.

## Rules (re-check each offseason)

| Step | Claim |
|---|---|
| `p6-rules-01`, `p6-rules-03`, `p6-rules-04` | NFL catch: two feet in bounds; college: one. NFL runner must be touched down; college runner is down when a knee touches. |
| `p6-rules-02`, `p6-rules-05` | NFL hash marks are narrower than college's. NFL DPI is a spot foul; college DPI is capped at 15 yards. |

## Pre-snap reads

| Step | Note |
|---|---|
| `p6-presnap-02`, `-05` | "More blockers than box defenders: run; fewer: throw" is the textbook rule; quarterback-run and RPO offenses change the arithmetic. |
